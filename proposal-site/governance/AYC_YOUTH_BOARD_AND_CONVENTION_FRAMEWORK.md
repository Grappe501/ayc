# AYC Youth Board of Directors and Annual Leadership Convention
Status: Proposed governance framework for ArkCAN board consideration, November 2026. Not adopted.

## 1. Purpose and legal structure
Arkansas Youth Coalition (AYC) is proposed as a permanent, internally administered program of Arkansas Civic Action Network (ArkCAN). The **AYC Youth Board of Directors** is the public-facing name of a youth governance council created under an ArkCAN-approved program charter. It is not a separate corporation or the legal board of ArkCAN. ArkCAN retains legal, financial, employment, charitable compliance, data-protection, and fiduciary authority. Youth directors exercise real, written delegated authority over program planning, member engagement, recommendations, convention agenda, and approved budget priorities.

## 2. Founding board: campaign-to-institution transition
The first youth board is appointed for a defined transitional term, proposed as 12 months. Candidates are drawn from students and young adults who worked with AYC during its campaign-era organizing and demonstrated sustained contribution, trust, collaboration, inclusive leadership, and commitment to nonpartisan civic participation. No candidate is approved solely for partisan campaign activity or candidate preference. Recommendations will be documented individually with evidence of leadership, role fit, eligibility, conflict disclosures, and willingness to serve. ArkCAN's authorized leadership approves appointments under the final charter. Names remain **TBD** pending nominations, vetting, consent, and approval.

## 3. Proposed divisions and elected seats
The final number of seats will be set after a capacity review. Proposed role-specific divisions:
1. Youth Board Chair — convenes meetings, represents the youth board, coordinates with ArkCAN.
2. Vice Chair / Regional Organizing — supports statewide chapter and campus coordination.
3. Voter Registration — leads compliant, nonpartisan registration assistance.
4. Voter Education & GOTV — leads neutral voting information, participation reminders, and volunteer training.
5. Campus & Community Outreach — develops college, trade-school, workforce, and community relationships.
6. Events & Annual Convention — manages youth gatherings, programming, and convention planning.
7. Communications & Creative Media — oversees approved storytelling, design, and youth communications.
8. Development & Fundraising — coordinates youth-led fundraising with ArkCAN staff and financial controls.
9. Membership & Volunteer Experience — supports recruitment, onboarding, accessibility, and retention.
10. Data, Evaluation & Technology — oversees measurement proposals and privacy-conscious tool use, with no independent access to protected data absent authorization.

Roles can be combined, divided, or reserved for at-large representation after the charter is approved. Each role requires a written description, qualifications, expected workload, training, and reporting obligations.

## 4. Annual youth leadership convention
AYC proposes an annual in-person or hybrid convention funded through a dedicated annual fundraising plan and ArkCAN-approved budget. It serves as both an educational gathering and a democratic leadership transition. Activities include an annual impact report, civic training, program forums, division-specific candidate presentations, elections, youth resolutions, and onboarding of incoming directors.

The convention must have a published membership eligibility date, voter roll, nomination period, candidate eligibility standards, neutral election administration, accessible participation and voting, clear vote-counting procedures, dispute resolution, and documented certification of results. Avoid pay-to-vote or fundraising-based eligibility. Any voting technology and personal-data use must pass privacy and security review. Youth participation must not depend on political affiliation or candidate preference.

## 5. Election and transition model (draft)
- **90 days before convention:** publish roles, eligibility, nomination rules, election timetable, budget, and accessibility accommodations.
- **60 days before:** close nominations and verify eligibility, conflicts, and consent.
- **30 days before:** publish candidate statements and a fair equal-access forum schedule.
- **Convention:** elect each role separately using an approved voting method; independent tellers certify totals.
- **Within 30 days afterward:** transfer records, train new directors, assign mentors, and adopt an annual work plan.

Proposed terms: one year; one vote per eligible member per elected seat; reasonable consecutive-term limits to be decided. Vacancies, ties, recalls, resignations, removal for cause, and election challenges must be specified before the first election. No election should proceed without a formally adopted rulebook.

## 6. Membership, age, and safeguards
The primary civic participation audience is ages 18–24. Eligibility for leadership and voting at the convention should be specified in the charter, including how currently enrolled students and young adults who age out are treated. If members under 18 participate in any AYC youth activities, require distinct minor safeguarding and parental-consent rules where applicable. ENGAGE student records cannot be transferred into AYC membership or voter outreach without separate lawful authorization and consent.

## 7. Finance and compliance
All convention income, donations, sponsorships, expenditures, contracts, and acknowledgments are administered through ArkCAN. AYC youth directors may develop and execute fundraising plans only within delegated approval limits. Funds restricted for the convention must be accounted for properly. Nonpartisan 501(c)(3) restrictions apply to programming, speakers, sponsorships, voter outreach, and candidate activity; counsel should review election and private-foundation grant rules.

## 8. Accountability
Publish an annual youth board report including approved financial summary, training hours, volunteer activity, registration-assistance outputs where lawful to report, participation measures, accessibility improvements, and lessons learned. Do not publish protected student or voter information. Require youth director orientation, conduct standards, conflict disclosures, quarterly reporting, and an ArkCAN liaison.

## 9. Decisions still required
- Initial appointment committee and list of founding nominees.
- Age and membership eligibility; campus vs community representation.
- Number of seats, divisions, term limits, and vacancy rules.
- Election method, ballot secrecy, voter verification, appeals, and recounts.
- Convention date, venue, accessibility, fundraising target, and budget.
- ArkCAN approval authority, delegated powers, spending limits, and charter amendments.

## 10. Requested board action
Authorize development of a formal AYC Youth Board charter and annual convention election code for ArkCAN review. Approve no individual appointment, transfer of funds, or legal governance change by implication from this draft.
